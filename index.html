Connecting Krisp (optional)

1

Copy your webhook URL

Open Krisp integration at the top of Pathfinder. Under Connection setup, press Copy connection link.

2

Open Krisp's webhook settings

In Krisp, go to Settings, then Integrations, then Webhook.

3

Choose Transcript created and save

Name the webhook Pathfinder and paste the entire copied link into Webhook URL. Under Trigger Events, select Transcript created only, then save. Leave the other events unchecked. Keep the link private; no extra headers or secrets are needed.

4

Return to Pathfinder and press ARM KRISP

Send a test lesson from Krisp. The first received transcript confirms the connection. Krisp stays armed for later lessons until you press DISARM KRISP.
