How to use Classbuster

A quick guide for teachers. No tech skills needed.

1

Pick a protocol

Classic is the default full report. Use the other modes when you want a different voice or format.

2

Paste your lesson notes

Put the transcript, rough notes, or lesson summary into Krisp Notes. Messy notes are fine.

3

Add the basics

Teacher name, student name, rating, and optional special remarks are enough for a strong report.

4

Generate, read, send

Classbuster copies reports automatically. Always read once before sending to a parent or student.

Protocol cheat sheet

Classic

Full, balanced student feedback with vocabulary, grammar, homework, and stars.

Sugarcoat

Warmer, softer feedback for parents who prefer extra encouragement.

Fairy

A pink Sugarcoat subprotocol for younger students and more magical reports.

Blitz

Fast, short feedback when you need something clean right now.

Beida

Learning-content style output for platform-ready summaries.

OF / Preply

Trial lesson or private-tutor follow-up formats.

Connecting Krisp (optional)

1

Copy your webhook URL

In the Krisp Notes section, find "Your personal Krisp webhook URL" and press Copy.

2

Open Krisp's webhook settings

In Krisp, go to Settings, then Integrations, then Webhook.

3

Paste and save

Paste the URL you copied and save. No extra headers or secrets needed -- the link itself is your private key.

4

You're connected
