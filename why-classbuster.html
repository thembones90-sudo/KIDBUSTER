CLASSBUSTER / WHY TEACHERS USE IT

Your lesson notes, turned into a report you are proud to send.

Classbuster drafts clear, warm parent feedback from the notes you already take, in the report format your platform expects. You stay the author: every draft is yours to review, edit and approve.

Open ClassbusterShare beta feedback

What it does for you

  • Less time on paperwork

    Add your lesson notes and ratings, and get a structured report draft back instead of starting from a blank page after every class.

  • The format you already use

    Seven report protocols, including Classic and formats built around Preply, Beida and LingoAce workflows, with the sign-offs and structure each one expects.

  • Grounded in your lesson

    Drafts are built from the notes and ratings you provide, so your own observations about the lesson stay at the centre of the report.

  • Optional transcript import

    Connect Krisp if you want to, and a lesson transcript can fill in your notes. Importing never generates a report on its own.

  • You stay in control

    Classbuster writes a draft, not a decision. Review, correct and approve every report before it reaches a parent or platform.

  • Privacy by design

    Use first names or placeholders. New imported transcript bodies are set to expire 12 hours after we receive them. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How it works

  1. Choose your report format and rate the lesson.
  2. Add your notes, or import a transcript if you use Krisp.
  3. Generate, review and copy the draft into your platform.
