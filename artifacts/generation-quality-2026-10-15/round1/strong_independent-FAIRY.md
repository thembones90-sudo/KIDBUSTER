Grammar Points We Covered

Present simple for facts – Used to describe weather that is generally or currently true.
👉 "It is hot in summer." / "It rains a lot in autumn."

Self-correction in speaking – Adjusting your sentence mid-way shows strong awareness.
👉 "You need a coat — sorry, I need a coat and gloves."

When-clauses – Linking a condition to a result using "when."
👉 "When it's foggy, I can't see clearly." / "When it rains, I take my umbrella."
