Grammar Points We Covered

Past tense with "was" – Use "was" to describe weather or situations that happened yesterday or in the past.
👉 "Yesterday it was sunny and warm."
👉 "Last week it was very cold and foggy in the morning."

Giving a reason with "because" – Use "because" to explain why you think or feel something.
👉 "Winter is my favourite season because I like snow."
👉 "I like summer because the days are long and bright."

Using "when" to describe conditions – Start a sentence with "when" to describe what happens in a certain situation.
👉 "When it's foggy, I can't see very far."
👉 "When it rains, I always take my umbrella."

Self-correction – When you notice a mistake and fix it yourself, that is excellent language awareness.
👉 You said "you need" then quickly corrected to "I need" all on your own. That kind of attention is a real strength.

Describing seasons – Use "in" before a season name to say what conditions or events happen during that time.
👉 "In autumn, the leaves fall from the trees."
👉 "In summer, it is hot and the days feel longer."
