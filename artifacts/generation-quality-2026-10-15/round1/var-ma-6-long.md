Grammar Points We Covered:

1. Present tense for weather descriptions
   👉 It's raining today and it's very windy.
   👉 When it's foggy, I can't see far.

2. Past tense for weather (was/were)
   👉 Yesterday it was sunny.
   👉 Last week it was very cold.

3. Giving a reason with "because"
   👉 Winter is my favourite season because I like snow.
   👉 I take an umbrella because it's raining.

4. Self-correction in speech
   👉 "You need a coat — sorry, I need a coat and gloves."
   👉 Catching and fixing a mistake mid-sentence is a real skill.
