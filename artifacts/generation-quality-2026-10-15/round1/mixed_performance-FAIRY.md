Grammar Points We Covered

Simple past tense (irregular verbs) – Some verbs change completely in the past.
👉 "I went to the park." / "We ate dinner together."

Simple past tense (regular verbs) – Add -ed to most verbs.
👉 "The firefighter helped a family." / "The farmer worked all day."

Possessive apostrophe – Add 's to show something belongs to someone.
👉 "My grandma's house is cozy." / "The doctor's hospital is nearby."
