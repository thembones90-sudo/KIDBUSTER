Grammar Points We Covered:

1. Using "when" to build conditional time clauses
   👉 When it's foggy, I can't see the bus stop.
   👉 When it rains, I take an umbrella.

2. Self-correcting pronoun use (you vs. I)
   👉 I need a coat and gloves.
   👉 I wear a raincoat when it rains.
