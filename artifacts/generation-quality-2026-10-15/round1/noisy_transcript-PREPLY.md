Today's lesson with Kai focused on animals — names, descriptions, and what animals can and can't do. The main thing I noticed is that Kai is picking up vocabulary quickly and is willing to jump in and try, which is exactly the right instinct at this stage.

When Kai needed more structure, I modeled the sentence and Kai repeated it with support — for example, "It's a big elephant" and "It can't swim" both came after I provided the full form first. Toward the end, Kai described a monkey climbing trees with noticeably less prompting, which was a nice moment. Word order and sentence structure are the natural next step to work on — Kai has the words; it's now about putting them in the right sequence independently.

If you ever look at picture books together at home, simply naming animals and saying one thing they can do would be a great way to reinforce this.
