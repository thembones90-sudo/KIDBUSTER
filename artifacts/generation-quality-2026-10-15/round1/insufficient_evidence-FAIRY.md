Grammar Points We Covered

Using "wearing" – We use "wearing" to talk about clothes we have on right now.
👉 "I am wearing a jacket." / "She is wearing a skirt."

Articles "a" and "an" – Use "a" before consonant sounds and "an" before vowel sounds.
👉 "I have a hat." / "She has an orange shirt."

Describing clothes – We can describe clothes using colors or size.
👉 "He is wearing a blue jacket." / "I am wearing big shoes."
