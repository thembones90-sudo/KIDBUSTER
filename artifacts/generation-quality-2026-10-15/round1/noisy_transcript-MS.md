📚 Animals and What They Can Do

Key Vocabulary with Pronunciation & Notes:

Giraffe 🦒 – a tall animal with a long neck | Pronunciation: jih-RAF | Note: named the animal and described it with support
Monkey 🐒 – a playful animal that climbs | Pronunciation: MUNK-ee | Note: used in a sentence with support
Neck 🦒 – the long part connecting head to body | Pronunciation: NEK | Note: practiced as part of a full sentence
Climb 🌳 – to go up something | Pronunciation: KLYME | Note: used naturally to describe what a monkey does
Swim 🏊 – to move through water | Pronunciation: SWIM | Note: repeated correctly after practice

Pronunciation Focus

🗣 Swim → SWIM (not "swing")

Grammar & Sentence Practice

"It's a big elephant."
"The giraffe has a long neck."
"The monkey can climb trees."

Grammar Points We Covered

Adjective word order
👉 It's a big elephant.
👉 The giraffe has a long neck.

Can and can't for ability
👉 The monkey can climb trees.
👉 It can't swim.
