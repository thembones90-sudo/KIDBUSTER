Grammar Points We Covered

Word order in descriptions – adjectives come before the noun in English.
👉 It's a big elephant. It's a fast monkey.

"Can" and "can't" – use these to talk about ability.
👉 A monkey can climb. A giraffe can't swim.

Subject + verb + object – sentences need all three parts.
👉 The monkey can climb trees. The elephant has big ears.
