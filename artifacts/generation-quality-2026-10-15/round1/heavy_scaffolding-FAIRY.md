Grammar Points We Covered

Articles a / an – Use "a" before consonant sounds and "an" before vowel sounds.
👉 It's a banana. It's an apple.

"I like + noun" – Use this pattern to talk about foods you enjoy.
👉 I like juice. I like bananas.

"It's a / an + noun" – Use this to name and describe things around you.
👉 It's a banana. It's an apple.
