Strengths

Lily brought genuine energy and engagement to her trial lesson, adapting to the classroom environment quickly and participating with confidence throughout. When asked to describe community workers, she responded with complete, well-formed sentences — explaining, for example, that a doctor helps sick people in a hospital and that a farmer grows food and has animals — without needing prompting to expand her ideas. Her vocabulary for occupations was clearly solid, and she retrieved the right words without hesitation. Lily's reading of the firefighter passage was fluent and accurate, and she went a step further by adding her own observation about the character being brave, showing she was actively thinking about the text rather than simply decoding it. Her listening skills were also evident — she absorbed teacher corrections mid-lesson and applied them quickly in her next attempt, moving from "I go" to "I went" after a single model. Her willingness to keep trying and her generally positive attitude made her a pleasure to work with.

Areas for Improvement

Lily's main area for growth is the consistent use of past tense verb forms. During the lesson, she used present-tense verbs in past-tense contexts — for example, "we eat cake" and "Tom help a cat" — and while she responded well to correction in the moment, the pattern did not yet carry over independently from one sentence to the next. She would also benefit from more practice with possessive apostrophes in speaking, as "grandma's house" required a gentle reminder. With regular exposure and practice, these patterns are well within reach.

Learning Suggestions

Future lessons would benefit from dedicated practice with common irregular past-tense verbs — go/went, eat/ate, help/helped — through activities such as storytelling or picture-based narration, where Lily can use them in context rather than in isolation. Pairing this with short reading passages or simple writing tasks would give her meaningful repetition and help the forms become automatic over time.
