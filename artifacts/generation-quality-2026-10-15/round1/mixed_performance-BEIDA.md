LEARNING CONTENT:
Hi Lily, teacher Nora here! 🌟 Today we reviewed some great job words and what different people do — including a doctor, a farmer, and a firefighter. We talked about what each one does, read a short story about a brave firefighter named Tom, and practised describing past events using past tense verbs like "went," "ate," and "played." It was a fun and busy lesson!

GENERAL AND DETAILED PERFORMANCE:
Lily showed a solid grasp of jobs vocabulary this lesson, confidently explaining what a doctor and a farmer do using clear, complete sentences — "A doctor helps sick people in a hospital" and "A farmer grows food and has animals" were both accurate and well-formed. She also engaged enthusiastically with the reading activity, reading the firefighter story aloud and even adding her own idea ("He is brave"), which showed real initiative and creative thinking.

The area that needs more practice is past tense verb forms. Throughout the lesson, Lily consistently used present tense verbs where past tense was needed — for example, "I go," "we eat," and "we play" instead of "went," "ate," and "played." When teacher Nora provided the correct models, Lily was able to repeat some of them, though the correction didn't always carry through fully in the same response, and the pattern continued into her final answer ("Tom help a cat"). This is a very normal stage of language learning, and more regular practice forming and using simple past tense verbs — especially irregular ones like "go/went" and "eat/ate" — would help Lily build greater consistency and confidence with this structure.

Keep it up, Lily — you are working hard and your vocabulary is really shining! 🌱💪
