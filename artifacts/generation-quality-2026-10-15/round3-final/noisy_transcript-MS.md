Today's Lesson:
📚 Animals and What They Can Do

Key Vocabulary with Pronunciation & Notes:

Elephant 🐘 – a very big animal with a trunk | Pronunciation: EL-uh-funt | Note: practiced in a sentence with support
Giraffe 🦒 – a tall animal with a long neck | Pronunciation: jih-RAF | Note: identified and used in a sentence with support
Monkey 🐒 – an animal that loves to climb | Pronunciation: MUN-kee | Note: used in a describing sentence with support
Neck 🦒 – the long part connecting head to body | Pronunciation: NEK | Note: completed a sentence using this word
Climb 🌳 – to go up something | Pronunciation: KLYME | Note: used in a sentence about the monkey

Pronunciation Focus

🗣 swim → SWIM (rhymes with "him")
🗣 can't → KANT (one short, clear sound)

Grammar & Sentence Practice

"It's a big elephant."
"The giraffe has a long neck."
"The monkey can climb trees."

Grammar Points We Covered

Using "a" and "an" before nouns
👉 It's a giraffe.
👉 It's an elephant.

Can / Can't for ability
👉 The monkey can climb trees.
👉 The giraffe can't swim.

Adjectives before nouns
👉 It's a big elephant.
👉 The giraffe has a long neck.
