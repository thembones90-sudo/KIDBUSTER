Ava found today's lesson challenging. Completing full sentences such as "It's an apple" was difficult, and she often said only part of the phrase even with modeling. Short, playful repetition will help these words feel more comfortable over time. Ava did keep trying throughout the lesson, and that willingness is the most important foundation to build on. 💛
