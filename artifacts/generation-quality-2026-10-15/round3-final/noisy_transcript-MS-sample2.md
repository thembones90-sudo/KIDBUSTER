swim 🏊 – to move through water | Pronunciation: SWIM | Note: drilled with can't in a full sentence

Pronunciation Focus

🗣 swim → SWIM (one syllable, short i sound)
🗣 giraffe → jih-RAF (stress on the second part)

Grammar & Sentence Practice

"It's a big elephant."
"The giraffe has a long neck."
"It can't swim."

Grammar Points We Covered

Adjective order (adjective before noun)
👉 It's a big elephant.
👉 It's a tall giraffe.

Can and can't for ability
👉 The monkey can climb trees.
👉 It can't swim.
