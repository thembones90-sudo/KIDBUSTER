Strengths

Lily began the lesson with clear, confident responses during the vocabulary review. When asked about jobs, she produced well-formed sentences independently — noting that a doctor helps sick people in a hospital and that a farmer grows food and keeps animals. These answers showed good retention of topic vocabulary and a natural ability to recall and use content words in context. Her reading of the firefighter passage was fluent and accurate, and she added her own unprompted comment — "He is brave" — which showed genuine engagement with the text beyond what was required. Lily followed along attentively throughout the lesson and responded willingly to each prompt, which reflects a positive attitude toward learning.

Areas for Improvement

The main area for development during this lesson was past tense verb forms. When speaking about her own weekend, Lily used present-tense forms — "go" and "eat" — where past tense was needed. After correction, she incorporated "went" and "ate" with support, though "played" required an additional prompt. In a similar response near the end of the lesson, she used "help" where "helped" was needed, suggesting that applying regular and irregular past tense forms in spontaneous speech is an area that would benefit from continued practice. Possessive forms also came up briefly — "grandma's" was modeled by the teacher — and this is another small area worth revisiting.

Learning Suggestions

Future lessons would benefit from regular, structured practice with past tense verbs — both irregular forms and regular "-ed" endings — through guided speaking and short writing activities. Revisiting possessives in context would also reinforce a pattern that appeared in this lesson. Building on Lily's evident enthusiasm for storytelling could be a natural and effective way to develop both of these skills.
